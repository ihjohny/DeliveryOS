# 05 — Merchant & Vendor Portal Specification

This document defines the interface layout and operational rules for the **Vendor Store Portal** (`/vendor`), a responsive React.js SPA running on tablets, desktops, and mobile browsers.

---

## 1. Interface Layout & Routes

```
/vendor
├── /live-orders      # Kitchen / Store Order Console (KDS)
├── /catalog          # Category, Product & Variant Management
├── /timings          # Operating Hours & Emergency Pause
└── /financials       # Sales Ledger & Settlement History
```

---

## 2. Module 1: Live Kitchen Order Console (`/live-orders`)

### Screen Behavior & Audio Alert Engine:
- **Audio Alarm**: Plays continuous looped chime (`/sounds/order-alarm.mp3`) upon receiving `order:new` via WebSocket. The audio context unlocks on first user interaction with the dashboard.
- **Visual Alert**: Flashing green badge on new order cards.
- **Acknowledgement**: Alarm silences only when staff taps **Accept** or **Reject**.

### Order Lanes (Kanban Layout):
1. **New Orders Lane**:
   - Order Card: `#OrderNumber`, items, special instructions, total amount, payment type.
   - Action 1: **ACCEPT** with prep time dropdown (`15m`, `20m`, `30m`, `45m`).
   - Action 2: **REJECT** with reason dropdown (`Kitchen Busy`, `Out of Stock`, `Closing Early`).
2. **In Preparation Lane**:
   - Displays remaining countdown timer based on chosen prep time.
   - Action: **READY FOR PICKUP** button. (Triggers dispatch broadcast to nearby riders).
3. **Ready for Pickup Lane**:
   - Shows assigned rider name, contact phone, and ETA.
   - Action: **HANDED TO RIDER** button.

---

## 3. Module 2: Catalog & Stock Management (`/catalog`)

### Category Management:
- Create, rename, re-order, and delete store categories.

### Product Management:
- Fields: Title, Description, Base Price, Unit Type (`piece`, `kg`, `500g`), Image URL.
- Sub-entities:
  - **Variants**: Name (e.g. Size/Weight) and price delta (`+/- X`).
  - **Add-on Groups**: Title, min selection, max selection, individual add-on items with pricing.

### Instant Stock Availability Toggle:
- Every product and variant has an active **`In Stock / Out of Stock`** toggle switch.
- Toggling instantly updates `is_in_stock` in the database via `PATCH /vendor/products/:id/stock` and updates customer apps in real-time.

---

## 4. Module 3: Store Operations (`/timings`)

- **Weekly Schedule**: Set `open_time` and `close_time` per day (Sunday through Saturday). Checkbox for `is_closed`.
- **Emergency Store Pause**: Quick button to pause incoming orders for `30 minutes`, `1 hour`, or `Rest of Day`.

---

## 5. Module 4: Sales & Ledgers (`/financials`)

- **Daily Summary**: Total orders today, Gross Sales, Platform Commission (e.g., 15%), Net Balance.
- **Settlement Statements**: Historical statements generated by the Super Admin showing payout statuses (`PENDING`, `SETTLED`).

---

## 6. Super Admin Central Authority Note

All vendor settings, menus, prices, and operating hours can be viewed, modified, or disabled directly by a `SUPER_ADMIN` from the central console (`/admin`).
